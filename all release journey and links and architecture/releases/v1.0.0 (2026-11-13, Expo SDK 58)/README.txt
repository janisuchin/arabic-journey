WHAT'S IN IT
- 90 daily lessons in 13 weekly modules with a test each week (pass mark 80%)
  and a final exam.
- Letters lab (31), pronunciation lab (30 sounds), 78 grammar guides, numbers
  lab, time lab, speaking lab, sentence explorer.
- 32 real-life scenarios with outlined licensed photos; 20 placard sets; 90
  real-life photo cards; Explore the Arab world (9 chapters); 202 word photos.
- 809 course words; fully vowelled Arabic with a transliteration under every
  word; tap any word for its meaning.
- Audio is the phone's own Arabic text-to-speech voice (not a native recording).

CONTENT STATUS
