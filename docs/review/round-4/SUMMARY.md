## What round 4 found and fixed
- **Major (3 applied):** day 82 and day 87 "if" answers missing fa- (فَسَنَحْتَفِلُ, فَقُلْ: the course's own rule);
  the jamal card said جَمَل and جَمِيل have different roots (both j-m-l).
- **House-style endings not yet shown** (the biggest group): inna/anna/lākinna nouns (days 61, 63, 67, 68, 69,
  office-phone scenario; plus سَامِيَ on day 61 by lead decision), kāna/laysa predicates (days 44, 46, 72), adverbial
  سَاعَةً وَاحِدَةً (day 48).
- **Answer keys:** day 74 quizzes 1–2 had a distractor that also made correct Arabic (now أَنْ); day 39 quiz option
  كَانَ → كَانُوا, and the day 39 note softened ("usually كَانَتْ").
- **Spelling:** فَلُوكَة (felucca; was fulūka), الْحَلَوِيَّات; day 89 quiz prompt quotes its لٰكِنَّ.
- **Rules and notes:** the يُمْكِنُ card ("object ending, as on any verb"), the مَا waṣla note, the present stem
  ـكْتُب (no mood vowel), bināya, ajid-hā, day 73 headline "beats".
- **Tap glosses** (scoped to their card/lesson unless right everywhere): يُحِبُّ forms, فَعَلَ, ضَحِكَ, طَوِيل "tall",
  تَشْرَبِي, أَذْهَبْ, يَنْزِلُ "falls" (snow), الْأُوبِرَا, يَسْتَعْمِلُ, تُسَبِّبُ, تُحِبُّ week 3; hand glosses
  إِنَّنِي, غَائِمَاتٌ, غَائِمِينَ, مِنِ (no answer give-away), تَأْتِي; يَرْجُو vocabulary sense.
- **Engine** (`src/core/translit.ts`, `src/lang/ar/tap.ts`, unit test "round 4"): a lone hamza seat reads ʾ; أُولٰئِكَ
  reads ulāʾika (silent wāw); a lone ؟ / ، in English prose stays Arabic (the card about the Arabic question mark);
  a run of bare letters is a comma list of names; bi-jāʾiza, bi-annanī, bi-milyūni hyphenated; li-ḥusni not
  capitalised as the name Hasan.
