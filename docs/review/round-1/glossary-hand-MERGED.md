# glossary-hand: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: Checked all 409 hand-glossary entries as dictionary glosses that override every tap app-wide, plus each transliteration against its harakat. 36 errors: 23 major, 13 minor. The dominant pattern (24 findings) is a sentence-specific '(wrong here — correct: …)' note used as the only gloss of a form that is perfectly valid elsewhere (أَحَد 'one', يَزُورُونَ, نَجَحُوا, أُعْلِنَ, بَنَتِ, the indicatives أَجِدُهَا/أَجْلِسُ/تُوقِظُنَا/أُسَجِّلُ, the nominatives الضَّجِيجُ/الطَّرِيقُ/الْمِفْتَاحُ/الْأَشْهَرُ, the genitive الْأَخْبَارِ, the duals طَبَقَانِ/تَذْكِرَتَانِ, إِنْتَرْنِت); these should carry the plain meaning, with the exercise-specific correction moved to the exercise's own seg. Separate real gloss errors: ـُوا glossed only as the imperative 'you all' though it is also the past 'they' ending; تُعْطِي glossed 'you give (to a woman)' instead of 'to a man / she gives'; سَمَحْتُمْ glossed as present; ـُ glossed as an iḍāfa marker only; تَأْمِين missing 'insurance'. Engine: الِا… keys transliterate as 'Iliā…' (3 keys), نَفْي as nafī, أَسْهَلُ as 'Ashalu'. The non-word mistake entries (غُرْفَةُهُ, طَالِبَةَانِ, زَارْنَا …), the pronoun/case-ending series, the grammar terms and the month names are correct.

Reviewer B: Read all 409 hand-glossary entries as app-wide dictionary glosses and checked each transliteration against its harakat. 14 errors: 5 major (engine renders the article before hamzat-waṣl words as 'Iliā…' in 3 keys; أَحَد glossed only as a 'wrong' Sunday although it means 'one/someone'; ـُوا glossed only as the imperative 'you all' although it is the past 'they' ending in this course; تُعْطِي glossed as 'you (to a woman)' instead of 'you (to a man)/she'; صِينِيَّة 'tray' hides 'Chinese (f)') and 9 minor (24 valid forms carrying one-sentence 'wrong — correct' remarks, grouped; 'after a verb' as the rule for -ayni/-īna; -hā/-hu missing 'its'; تَأْمِين, سَمَحْتُمْ, تُسَاعِدَ, لِلْمَسْجِدِ, الْعَدَّاد narrowed or shifted; s-h/dh-h digraph ambiguity in the engine). The main pattern: the file mixes true dictionary glosses with mistakes-pair commentary ('wrong here', 'wrong after inna'), which is harmless on non-words but leaks onto correct forms wherever they occur. Vocabulary glosses, month names, number-gender rules, pronoun endings and grammar-term entries are otherwise accurate; 12 notes for the owner.

**Errors: 41** (9 found by both reviewers, 32 by one), 24 major. Notes: 25.
