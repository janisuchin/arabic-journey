# days-31-40: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: Checked all ten days (31-40): every Arabic string with its harakat and case endings, the segs and tap glosses, the English and literal lines, all 70 quiz answer keys, the mistakes right/wrong pairs, grammar statements in intros and why-lines, the pronunciation items and the transliteration against the Arabic. The Arabic itself is clean: عِنْدَ/لِ/مَعَ, لَيْسَ with the accusative, adjective agreement (including non-human plurals with feminine singular, عُيُونُهَا خَضْرَاء, اللَّيَالِي بَارِدَةً), the 3-10 / 11+ counting rules, the hollow verbs زُرْنَا/كُنَّا/نِمْتُ, كَانَ with the accusative predicate, and قَبْلَ/بَعْدَ (أَنْ) are all correct, and every quiz key is right. I found 5 errors: 2 major, both in the shared tap glossary rather than in the lesson text, where a word is glossed in a sense that contradicts the lesson (مَا shown as 'what' on the very sentences teaching it as 'not' on day 37; تَسْكُنُ shown as 'you live (to a man)' on the day-35 lines where it means 'she lives'); and 3 minor (ـًا glossed as 'the letter alif', the day-38 intro calling the -an ending '-a', and the engine writing shāī for الشَّاي). The pattern is that the glossary stores one sense per spelling, so any homograph (مَا, تَسْكُنُ, طَوِيل) shows the wrong sense whenever a lesson uses the other one; fixing those entries once would clear every occurrence.

Reviewer B: Read the whole of audit/days-31-40.txt (2,775 lines, days 31-40: عِنْدِي/لِي/مَعِي, describing people, لَيْسَ with the -an predicate, ages and counted nouns, kin terms, past tense, مَا-negation, time expressions, كَانَ, قَبْلَ/بَعْدَ). I checked every Arabic string, its harakat and case endings, agreement (adjective-noun, verb-subject, non-human plurals as f.sg., number polarity 3-10 vs 11+), the translations and literals, every seg and tap gloss, every quiz answer key and the transliteration. The Arabic itself is in good shape: all answer keys are right, the لَيْسَ/كَانَ accusative predicates, hollow-verb shortening (زُرْنَا), ثَمَانِي/عَشْرُ سَنَوَاتٍ, عَشَرَةُ أَحْفَاد, كَانَتِ اللَّيَالِي بَارِدَةً are all correct. I found 8 errors (2 major, 6 minor) and 8 notes. The two major findings are both gloss problems caused by the shared tap dictionary giving only one sense of a word: مَا is glossed 'what' in every negated sentence of day 37 (including the quiz item whose answer is 'I didn't swim'), and تَسْكُنُ is glossed 'you live (to a man)' where it means 'she lives' in day 35 (the exact he/she contrast the card and quiz are testing). The minor findings are the hamzat-qaṭʿ spelling الْإِثْنَيْن for Monday (should be waṣl), two more one-sense tap glosses (خَالَة without 'mother's sister', طَوِيل as only 'long', ـًا as 'the letter alif'), and two engine patterns: names after وَ/لِ lose their capital (Wadānyāl, wanūr), and bare letters/endings in prose get odd transliterations (ة → 'a / at', أ → empty, الشَّاي → sh-shāī). Pattern for the owner: the seg glosses written per sentence are reliable; the global tap dictionary needs multi-sense entries for function words and verb forms that are ambiguous (مَا, تَسْكُنُ and similar 2nd-m/3rd-f present forms).
