# vocabulary: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: Checked all 809 entries of audit/vocabulary.txt: headword spelling and harakat, English meaning, gender, plural, listed forms, example sentence (agreement, case after كَانَ/لَيْسَ/إِنَّ/أَنْ, numbers with counted nouns, iḍāfa) and its translation, and the generated transliteration and tap glosses. The Arabic itself is in very good shape: no misspelt headword, wrong gender, wrong plural, wrong conjugation or wrong example agreement was found, and the number/counted-noun forms (ثَلَاثَ مَرَّات, عَشْرُ سَنَوَاتٍ, خَمْسَةَ عَشَرَ ضَيْفًا, عِشْرُونَ جُنَيْهًا) are all correct. Only two minor orthographic points (مَقْهىً tanwīn position, انْتِبَاه without kasra). Almost every real problem is in the app-generated tap glosses, which are looked up by consonant skeleton or shared key: about twenty forms show another word's meaning (مُدَرِّسَة=school, كُتَّاب=book, سَادَة=plain, without sugar, يُكْتَبُ=writes), several show the wrong person or gender (سَلَامَتُكِ 'to a man', خُطَّتُكَ 'to a woman', سَبَحَتْ 'I swam', نَجَحْتُ 'you succeeded'), two glosses leak editorial annotations that are themselves wrong (يَزُورُونَ, الْأَخْبَارِ), and many carry grammar tags from another sentence ('after an', 'form after lam', 'after laysa'). Transliteration errors are minor and systematic (لِحًى → Liḥanā, رَأْي → Raʾī, أَسْهَلُ → ashalu, مِائَة → Miāʾa) plus the four known quirks. Totals: 16 errors (4 major, 12 minor), 11 notes.

Reviewer B: Read all 809 entries of audit/vocabulary.txt (17,983 lines) and checked headword spelling and harakat, English meaning, gender, plural, listed forms, example sentences with their translations and the tap glosses. The Arabic itself is in very good shape: I found no misspelt headword, no wrong plural or gender, no agreement or case error in the example sentences, and no wrong English translation of a sentence. Almost every error is in the tap-gloss layer, which is clearly auto-filled from a shared dictionary: (1) person/gender mismatches between a verb form and its gloss (أَحَبَّ 'I', أَشْبَهَ 'I look like', سَبَحَتْ 'I swam', سَلَامَتُكِ 'to a man', خُطَّتُكَ 'to a woman', نَجَحْتُ 'you', and the recurring تَـ-verb glossed 'you (to a man)' where the subject is a woman or vice versa); (2) a verb form glossed as a different word altogether (شَعَرَ=hair, دَرَّسَ=lesson, حَجَزَ=booking, تَقَدَّمَ=progress, مَنَعَ=banning, تَخَرَّجَ=graduation, كُتَّاب=book, سَادَة=plain coffee, مُدَرِّسَة=school, شَرِيحَة=SIM card, الْإِشَارَة=pointing); (3) past 'he' forms glossed as imperatives (تَنَفَّسَ, كَرَّرَ, اِسْتَعْمَلَ, عَبَّرَ, خَمَّنَ, انْتَبَهَ); (4) two reviewer comments that leaked into learner-facing glosses and are wrong where they now sit (saih يَزُورُونَ, nashra الْأَخْبَارِ); (5) minor mood brackets ('after an', 'after lam') attached to plain indicative forms and senses copied from another sentence. 45 findings in all: 29 major (all gloss) and 16 minor, the minor ones being 11 gloss, 1 Arabic (sentence-initial hamzat waṣl left unvowelled in three places) and 4 engine (the four known quirks confirmed, and additionally رَأْي→Raʾī, مِائَة→Miāʾa, لِحًى→Liḥanā, أَسْهَلُ→ashalu, ة after ā → 'h'). 16 notes for the owner.
