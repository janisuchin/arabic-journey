# scenarios-2: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: Checked all 16 scenarios (airport-checkin to school): every intro, culture note, dialogue line with its transliteration, English and segs, both mistakes pairs, all quiz items and answer keys, and every photo-scene spot and caption. The Arabic itself is in very good shape: case endings after إِنَّ / لٰكِنَّ / كَانَ / لَيْسَ, subjunctive after أَنْ and لَنْ, jussive after لَا / لَمْ, duals (تَذْكِرَتَيْنِ, وَالِدَا, الْوَالِدَيْنِ), diptotes (دَنَانِيرَ, صَنَادِيقَ, لُطَفَاءَ, أَسْوَدَ) and the number system (3–10 plural with gender polarity, 11–99 singular accusative, hundreds singular) are correct and consistently taught; all 64 quiz answer keys are right. 12 errors: 2 major (machines mistakes[0] marks the correct transitive اِضْغَطِ الزِّرّ as wrong and teaches a false rule; the phone-shop scene glosses the SIM-card label as 'the slide'), 7 minor tap-gloss problems where the dictionary sense shown does not match the object or sentence (أَفْضَلُ, حَضْرَةَ, سَيِّدَات, الرَّصِيف, الْعَرَبَة, الْعَمُود, السَّاعَة), and 3 engine items (the known quirks once each, plus a new one: وَبِالنِّسْبَةِ → 'Wabiālnnisbati', وَلِلرَّجُلِ → 'walilrrajuli'). The pattern is that scene-spot tap glosses come from a generic dictionary rather than the scene context. 11 notes cover inconsistencies (صَبَاحَ/صَبَاحُ, hamzat al-waṣl marking), photo/scenario location mismatches and clumsy English.

Reviewer B: Read all 3,091 lines of audit/scenarios-2.txt (16 scenarios: airport-checkin, hotel-reception, job-interview, office-phone, eid-visit, cinema, bank, lost-phone-help, museum-info, post-office, supermarket, phone-shop, dietary, etiquette, machines, school, each with intro, dialogue, mistakes, quiz and photo-scene spots) and checked the Arabic, harakat, case endings, agreement, verb moods, number-noun syntax, translations, seg/tap glosses, answer keys, mistakes pairs and transliteration. The Arabic itself is in very good shape: dual forms (وَالِدَا سَامِي دَعَوَانِي, تُرِيدَانِ أَنْ تَجْلِسَا), diptotes (إِلَى دَنَانِيرَ, لُطَفَاءَ, دَقَائِقَ), numbers (ثَلَاثِمِئَةٍ وَخَمْسِينَ دِينَارًا, عَشْرِ دَقَائِق, اِثْنَيْ عَشَرَ جُنَيْهًا), إِنَّ after قَالَ and subjunctives after أَنْ/لَنْ are all correct, every quiz answer key and every mistakes right/wrong pair checks out except one over-strict rule (machines: اِضْغَطِ الزِّرّ treated as wrong). I found 0 major and 15 minor errors: 1 small case-ending slip in a standalone quiz phrase (رَقْمِ التَّتَبُّع), 1 questionable grammar rule, 9 tap-gloss sense mismatches on scene spots and culture notes (the SIM card شَرِيحَة glossed 'slide', سَيِّدَات '(toilets)', حَضْرَة 'sir' for a woman, مَكْتَب/عَرَبَة/رَصِيف/عَمُود/سَاعَة glossed with the wrong sense for the pictured object, أَفْضَلُ 'the best' where it means 'better'), and 4 engine findings (the known 'A / at', 'liāstiqbāl' and 'sh-shāī' quirks plus a new one: wa+li+al / wa+bi+al clusters rendered 'walilrrajuli' / 'Wabiālnnisbati'). The main pattern is that scene-spot tap glosses are drawn from a word-level dictionary and do not always match the object in the photo; the dialogue segs, by contrast, are consistently context-correct. Eight notes cover the صَبَاحَ/صَبَاحُ inconsistency, سُوق gender, photo-location mismatches and minor gloss wording.
