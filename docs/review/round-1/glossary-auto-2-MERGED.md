# glossary-auto-2: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: Checked all ~1,700 entries of the second half of the auto glossary (س to ي) as dictionary glosses for an unvocalised key. 45 findings: 18 major and 27 minor (five of the minor ones are grouped entries covering about 80 keys). The dominant pattern is one-sided glossing: a key that covers two different words gets only the reading of the sentence it was harvested from (يعلم = teaches but not knows; عرفنا = introduce to us! but not we knew; كتبه = he wrote it vs كتبها = her books; وجدته = and his grandmother vs وجدتها = I found it; ظهرا = back but not at noon; عبر = express! but not across). Second pattern: past verbs in -ت and present verbs in ت- glossed with a single person or gender from one sentence (قلت = you said (to a woman) when I said is the default; ناموا and وتكلم glossed only as imperatives though the past reading is identical). Third: editorial notes leaked into glosses, chiefly '(after an / lam)' on forms whose spelling is also the plain indicative, and 'will pass' on the past نجحت. Noun/verb homographs (وصور, عملنا, وأقدم, وفرنا, وبني) round out the majors. The rest of the chunk is accurate; number phrases, prepositional la-/li- compounds, names and place names were sound.

Reviewer B: Read all ~1,700 entries of audit/glossary-auto-2.txt (س–ي) as dictionary glosses for unvocalised keys. 40 findings: 17 major, 23 minor. The main patterns: (1) person taken from one sentence rather than from the word: singular verbs glossed '(they)' (سيخرج, وسيأتي, يصوم), -hā glossed 'them' (عنها, قالها, يقرؤها), and a systematic one-person gloss on every past-tense -ت verb (شربت = 'I drank' only, قلت = 'you said (to a woman)' only, وأخذت = 'she took' only) across more than 40 keys; (2) one-sided glosses where the spelling covers two common words: عمان Amman/Oman, ظهرا back/at noon, يعلم teaches/knows, علمت taught/knew, عرفنا introduce!/we knew, عبر express!/through, صنع was made/made, ولد boy/was born, وجدته and his grandmother/I found it, عام, كريم, وقع, وصفة, والتأمين, والوزن, والكبار; (3) small leaks of sentence context or engine notes into the gloss (غطيت '-i links to the next word', نجحت 'will pass', مضبوط 'medium sweet', يصوم 'verb first: singular'); (4) the -ك gender label '(to a man)' vs '(to a woman)' is a convention that the key cannot support (minor, 7 inconsistent entries). The and-/for-/the- compound entries (و-, ل-, لل-, وال-), numbers, food, names, months and most adjectives were checked and are correct.

**Errors: 59** (26 found by both reviewers, 33 by one), 24 major. Notes: 21.
