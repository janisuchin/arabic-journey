# grammar: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: I read all 78 grammar guides (summary, formula, explanation, tables, examples, translations, transliterations and tap glosses) against MSA. The grammar rules themselves are sound: the taught forms (nisba, iḍāfa, numbers 3–10 vs 11–99 with polarity, kāna/laysa/inna predicates, لَمْ/لَنْ/أَنْ moods, imperatives, hollow verbs, passive vowelling, dual and sound plurals, conditionals) are correct, and the Arabic strings and harakat are almost entirely right; I found one Arabic case error (الْيَوْمَ as the subject of 'today is the fifth') and one rule stated too loosely ('after a verb' for the -īna / -ayni endings). The real weakness is the tap-gloss layer, which is context-blind: 34 errors in total, 9 major and 25 minor. The major ones are all wrong tap glosses that contradict the guide they sit in (مُدَرِّسَة=school, دَرَّسَ=lesson, أَفْعَلَ=I do, اِسْتَعْمَلَ=use (to a man), كَرِيم=Karim in رَمَضَان كَرِيم, يُكْتَبُ=writes, صَنَعَ/اِكْتَشَفَ glossed as passives, تَفْتَحُ=(it) is opened, and مَا=what throughout the 'didn't' guide). The minor ones are glosses taken from another sentence's sense (إِنَّ, مُنْذُ, أَحْسَنُ, حَاضِر, endings glossed as letter names) plus five engine transliteration quirks reported once each. 14 notes cover simplifications, clumsy machine glosses and style.

Reviewer B: Checked all 78 grammar guides in audit/grammar.txt (summary, formula, explanation, every table cell and example, with their transliterations and tap glosses) against MSA. The grammar taught is sound: the rules on agreement, non-human plurals, numbers 3-10 vs 11-99 (reverse gender, plural vs singular -an), iḍāfa, لَيْسَ/كَانَ accusative predicates, the moods after لَمْ / لَنْ / أَنْ, the imperative helper vowels, hollow verbs, the conditional particles and the passive vowelling are all correct, and the Arabic forms and harakat in the formulas, tables and examples are right with one minor case slip (الْيَوْمَ for الْيَوْمُ in the dates example). 22 errors in total, 9 major and 13 minor, and 16 notes. Nearly every error is in the tap-gloss layer, not in the Arabic: the glossary is one entry per spelling, so (a) homographs get the wrong sense (مُدَرِّسَة=school, ذَهَب=gold, دَرَّسَ=lesson, كَرِيم=Karim in رَمَضَان كَرِيم), (b) active and passive verbs share a gloss in the very lesson that contrasts them (صَنَعَ/اِكْتَشَفَ 'was made/discovered', يُكْتَبُ 'writes', تَفْتَحُ 'is opened'), and (c) the ambiguous ta- forms (you-m / she) are glossed with one person and so contradict the translation in about seven sentences. The owner's quickest win is a double gloss for every 2m/3f present form and for the particles مَا / لَا / إِنَّ / مُنْذُ, plus the handful of homograph fixes listed.

**Errors: 35** (21 found by both reviewers, 14 by one), 12 major. Notes: 30.
