# scenarios-1: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: I read all 16 scenarios in audit/scenarios-1.txt (cafe, taxi, morning-routine, appointment-time, restaurant, bakery, directions, clothes-shop, renting-flat, family-visit, lost-luggage, holiday-story, doctor, pharmacy-visit, birthday-invitation, weather-plans) in full: every intro, speaker name, culture note, dialogue line with its English and segs, every mistakes right/wrong pair and its 'why', every quiz answer key and option, and every photo-scene caption, spot label, note and tap gloss. The Arabic itself is in very good shape: harakat, case endings (accusative after كَانَ/لَيْسَ, -an objects, يَوْمَ, مُنْذُ يَوْمَيْنِ), number–noun agreement (ثَلَاثَ كَعْكَات, خَمْسَةُ أَرْغِفَة, عَشْرُ دَقَائِق, سِتّ سَنَوَات, بِعَشَرَة جُنَيْهَات vs خَمْسَةَ عَشَرَ جُنَيْهًا), moods after لَمْ/لَنْ/أَنْ, feminine and dual imperatives, the kasra on كَمِ/هَلِ/تَفَضَّلِ before al- and the non-human plural → feminine singular rule are all correct, and all quiz answer keys and mistakes pairs check out (the bakery arithmetic 10+30+30=70, change 30 is also right). I found 11 errors: 1 major and 10 minor. The one major finding is a wrong tap gloss on the corrected form in the holiday-story mistakes card (طَوِيلَةً glossed 'tall (f, after is not)' instead of 'long (f, after was)'). The pattern behind the minor findings is the shared tap-gloss dictionary: scene spot glosses that pick another sense of the word than the one in the photo or sentence (رَصِيف 'platform' for a pavement, عَمُود 'pole' for a pillar/column, سَاعَة 'hour' for a wall clock, إِشَارَة 'pointing' for a traffic light, حَارّ 'hot (weather)' for a chili), plus spurious '(of)' on مَقْهَى and مُوَظَّفَة. The four engine quirks named in the brief (bare ة → 'A / at', capitalised name after a clitic, شَاي → 'shāī', kasra-alif words → 'liāntiẓār'/'Iliāstiqbāl') each occur and are reported once. 8 notes cover English wording, one أَوْ/أَمْ register point, the Petra photo for a Luxor story and a 'counter' gloss.

Reviewer B: Read the whole scenarios-1 export (16 scenarios: intros, dialogues, mistakes, quizzes and all photo-scene spots) line by line, checking harakat, case and tanwīn, gender/number agreement, verb moods after لَمْ / لَنْ / أَنْ, number–noun rules, every English line, every seg and tap gloss, every answer key and every scene label. The core Arabic is in good shape: all answer keys and right/wrong pairs are correct, dual, jussive and subjunctive forms are right, the number rules (3–10 plural, 11–99 singular -an, مِئَة + singular, feminine ثَلَاث / سِتّ) are applied correctly throughout. Fourteen findings: one major (the tap gloss on the model answer طَوِيلَةً reads 'tall (f, after is not)'), nine minor content issues that are almost all tap glosses pulled from a shared dictionary and mismatching the scene or sentence (الْإِشَارَة 'pointing', الرَّصِيف 'platform', الْعَمُود 'pole', السَّاعَة without 'clock', حَارّ 'weather', spurious '(of)', لَا always 'no'), one added 'very' in a translation, one inconsistent tanwīn on tāʾ-marbūṭa objects against the course's own taught rule, and four engine quirks reported once each. Twelve notes cover register, Egyptian vs Levantine vocabulary and style.

**Errors: 17** (8 found by both reviewers, 9 by one), 1 major. Notes: 20.
