# letters-pronunciation: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: Checked all 31 letter entries (names, Arabic names, IPA, transliteration symbols, sun/moon status, sound descriptions, example words and glosses) and all 30 pronunciation pages (IPA, letters, tips, diagrams, common-error texts, examples and every minimal pair). Letter names, sun/moon assignments (14/14), IPA values and transliteration symbols are all correct; every minimal pair is a real pair with correct harakat and the .en glosses of the pairs are right. 5 errors: 2 major, 3 minor. The one systematic problem is the tap-gloss engine, which matches words without harakat/shadda, so on the minimal-pair pages the second word of the pair (كَاتَبَ, دَرَّسَ, جَمَال, حَجَزَ, ظُهْر) is glossed as the first word or an unrelated noun — exactly the confusion the pages are meant to remove. The only content error in the Arabic/English itself is هَال glossed 'halo' (it is cardamom), plus the mild 'humble' for ذَلِيل. Minor engine/gloss points: zāy/wāw transliterated Zāī/Wāū, and ل / و glossed as the particles 'for' / 'and' on the alphabet pages. Notes cover inconsistent tongue-position wording for the emphatics, English vowel anchors, the French-r comparison for ghayn, and garbled ⟶ lines where tips contain bare diacritics.

Reviewer B: Checked all 31 letter entries (Latin and Arabic names, sun/moon flag, IPA, transliteration symbol, sound description, example word and gloss) and all 30 pronunciation pages (IPA, letters lists, tips, diagrams, common-error texts, examples and every minimal pair for existence, meaning and harakat). The letter data itself is clean: names, sun/moon status (14/14), IPA and symbols are all correct, and every minimal pair is a real pair with the right vowels. The one major problem is systemic, not in the content: the tap-gloss engine matches words without their harakat, so the second word of several minimal pairs shows the meaning of the first (ظُهْر = 'back', كَاتَبَ = 'writer', دَرَّسَ = 'lesson' ×3, جَمَال = 'camel (plural)', حَجَزَ = 'booking'), which teaches exactly the confusion the pages are meant to prevent. Six minor errors: هَال glossed 'halo' (it is cardamom), the sīn/ṣād page describing the emphatic tongue as pulled 'down' (the other two emphatic pages say raised), the engine transliterating زَاي / وَاو as Zāī / Wāū, the letters ل and و tapping as 'for' / 'and', context-bound tap glosses (خَيْر 'good night', سَلَام 'wow!', السَّفَر 'passport', نُور 'Nour'), and the linking page's over-general 'never two consonants across words'. Eleven notes cover vowel-comparison wording, display artefacts for seated hamzas, and a few softer glosses.

**Errors: 11** (1 found by both reviewers, 10 by one), 3 major. Notes: 20.
