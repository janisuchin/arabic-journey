# culture: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: Checked all nine Explore chapters (calligraphy, coffee, desert, family, food, places, ramadan, science, souq): every Arabic title, body-text word, sentence, mistake pair, word entry, reading and quiz, with their harakat, case endings (diptotes إِيرَانَ / إِسْحَاقَ / مَلَابِسَ / دِمَشْقَ, accusative after كَانَ and لٰكِنَّ, number–noun gender in بِعَشَرَةِ رِيَالَاتٍ), agreement, verb forms, translations, literals, segs, tap glosses, answer keys and the generated transliteration. The Arabic itself is clean: I found no major error and no sentence a learner would copy wrongly. The 12 findings are all minor: 7 tap-gloss mismatches where a word shows the sense written for another sentence (كَرِيم as 'Karim (first name)' in رَمَضَان كَرِيم, يَد 'hand luggage', وَادِي 'Wadi Musa', elatives أَقْدَمُ/أَكْبَرُ/أَكْثَرُ fixed to one of their two senses, يَصُومُ, تَفَضَّلْ, بَارِدًا '(object)'), one vocalisation inconsistency (الْمَصْرِيّ vs the quiz's al-Miṣrī), and 4 engine transliteration quirks reported once each (shāī, final -w as ū, no waṣla elision after tanwīn, capitalisation of names after وَ and al-). All quiz and reading answer keys are correct. 14 notes for the owner cover a vocative vowel, the gender of سُوق, the Khwārizmī vowel, a few culture/caption details and harmless display quirks.

Reviewer B: Checked all nine Explore chapters (calligraphy, coffee, desert, family, food, places, ramadan, science, souq): every Arabic title, body-text Arabic, example sentence with literal and segs, mistakes pairs, words list, reading and its segs, reading questions and quizzes with answer keys, and the engine transliteration. The Arabic is of a high standard: case after kāna, diptotes (صَحْرَاءَ, مَلَابِسَ, إِسْحَاقَ, مِصْرَ, دِمَشْقَ), accusative أَبَا الْهَوْل, numbers 3-10 and 11-99, verb-first singular agreement and non-human plural agreement are all correct, and every answer key and mistakes pair is right. 12 errors: 1 major (الْمَصْرِيّ for مِصْرِيّ in family.json, with an internal inconsistency against the quiz's 'al-Miṣrī'), 11 minor: two vocalisation gaps in places.json (missing tanwīn on the noun after a superlative, missing case vowels on sentence subjects), seven tap-gloss problems where the single-sense tap dictionary shows a sense from another sentence (وَادِي 'Wadi Musa', يَد 'hand luggage', كَرِيم as a first name in رَمَضَان كَرِيم, يَصُومُ 'they fast' on the isolated verb, الْأَكْبَر 'biggest' for eldest, بَارِدًا '(object)' for a kāna predicate, elatives glossed as superlative in comparatives and vice versa, تَفَضَّلْ 'here you are' beside prose saying 'come in'), and one engine finding (shāī, ḥulū/badū, Wabākistāna, ḥayāh). 13 notes on facts, English wording and consistency; no history or geography statement was found to be wrong.
