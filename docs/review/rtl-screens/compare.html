Arabic Journey — before / after the direction flip

Same build, same content, same screens (Pixel 7 width). Nothing in the Arabic changes: every Arabic word and sentence is still written and read right-to-left with the same font and vowel marks. What changes is only the frame around it: English, transliteration, arrows, tabs and progress bars follow English reading order, exactly like Nihongo Journey.

What to check on your own: (1) Arabic words look identical in both columns, only their position moves; (2) the exclamation/question marks sit at the end of the Arabic (its left side) — that is correct Arabic; (3) English and transliteration now start at the left and the Start/Next arrows point right; (4) the tab bar starts with Today on the left.
