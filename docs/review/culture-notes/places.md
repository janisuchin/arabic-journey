# places (cul.places, n 7) — open points for a teacher

- Burj Khalifa "tallest tower/building in the world" (pls11, Dubai body): true as of Nov 2026; Jeddah Tower (Saudi Arabia) is planned to pass it (~2028). Re-check before each release; 828 m is the usual rounded height.
- Dubai Creek: written خَوْرُ دُبَيّ (MSA vowelling khawr); locally pronounced khōr. Confirm the preferred vowelling.
- بَرَاجِيل (wind towers): Gulf word of Persian origin (bādgīr); the singular is given as bārjīl in a note. A teacher may prefer the MSA phrase بُرْجُ الرِّيَاح as well.
- عَبْرَة (abra): vowelling ʿabra follows common usage; confirm.
- Qarawiyyin: "often described as the oldest university in the world still working" (UNESCO/Guinness wording); founding date given as 859 (Fatima al-Fihri). Some sources give 857.
- Fez founded "at the end of the 8th century" (789, Idris I; Idris II 808); Marrakesh 1070 — so "Fez is older than Marrakesh" (pls8) holds.
- Petra: Treasury "carved about 2,000 years ago" (usually dated to the 1st century AD) and "probably the tomb of a Nabataean king" (often linked to Aretas IV) — both hedged.
- Giza: "about 4,500 years ago" (c. 2560 BC); "Memphis and its Necropolis" and Historic Cairo both UNESCO 1979; Petra 1985; Medina of Fez 1981. Dubai Creek is only on the UAE tentative list, so no World Heritage claim is made.
- Nile given as "one of the longest rivers" (not "the longest") on purpose, because of the Amazon debate.
- Case endings: like the days, many final nouns are in pause form (الْأَهْرَام, الْعَالَم), with full endings where they teach something (أَهْرَامَ الْجِيزَة, شَوَارِعُ الْمَدِينَةِ الْقَدِيمَةِ ضَيِّقَةٌ). Check consistency.
- pls13 is an opinion sentence (old Dubai more beautiful than new Dubai) — fine as personal taste, but a teacher may prefer a neutral fact.
