# culture: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: I read the whole culture chunk (10 chapters: calligraphy, coffee, desert, family, food, places, ramadan, science, souq) and checked every Arabic line: harakat, case after kāna and inna, verb-first agreement, diptotes (مِصْرَ, إِيرَانَ, صَحْرَاءَ, مَلَابِسَ, مَسْقَطَ), number–noun agreement (بِعَشَرَةِ رِيَالَاتٍ, ثَمَانِيَةٌ وَعِشْرُونَ حَرْفًا), segs, translations, the mistake cards and the quiz keys. The Arabic itself is very clean: no wrong answer key, no wrong translation and no wrong grammar rule. Every mistake card is correct, and the mistake-notes glossary has no culture entries. I found 14 errors (2 major, 12 minor). Ten are tap glosses (one gloss can cover several taps). The pattern: a tap picks a sense from another context. كَرِيم is shown as the name Karim inside رَمَضَان كَرِيم. أَقْدَمُ is shown as 'the oldest' where the lesson teaches 'older than', including on the correct quiz option. أَكْبَرُ is shown as 'bigger' in a superlative. مِنْ is shown only as 'from' in 'than' lines. الْعَرَبِيَّة is shown as 'the language' when it is an adjective. وَادِي carries a Wadi Musa gloss in the Wadi Rum line. Two more errors are missing tanwīn in the middle of a phrase (بُرْج, مَكَان), and two are engine issues (clitic + name 'Wabākistāna', Arabic-Indic digits turned into Western digits in the ⟶ line). Tap glosses checked: 876 entries in 324 tap lines; 24 of them were wrong or misleading in their line.
