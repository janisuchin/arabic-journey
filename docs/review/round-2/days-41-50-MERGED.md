# days-41-50: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: I read all of days 41-50 (2,732 export lines): every Arabic line, mistake card, quiz key, grammar note and transliteration, and all 302 tap lines (620 tap glosses), plus the segs in context. The Arabic itself is in very good shape. The lam + jussive forms (including -i before al-, lam arahu, lam yara), the an + subjunctive forms (an ashtariya, an tashrabī), the imperatives (iftaḥ, ibqa, irtaḥ, kul, khudh, nam, ilʿabā, lā tansaw), faʿlān feminines (ʿaṭshā, ghaḍbā), body-part gender, the numbers with counted nouns (thamānin wa-thalāthūna daraja, thalātha marrāt, miʾa wa-ʿishrūna junayhan, khamsa daqāʾiq) and verb-subject agreement in the Juha story are all correct, and every quiz answer key checks out. I found 7 errors (3 major, 4 minor), all in the tap/gloss layer or the engine. Of the 620 tap glosses, 25 are wrong. Most of these are لَا shown as 'no' where it means 'don't' or 'not' (16 taps, 14 of them on day 47, whose topic is the prohibition) and مَا shown as 'what' where it is the past negation on day 41 (3 taps). The rest are يَحْضُرُ glossed with a past meaning, two imperatives glossed as third-person statements ('plays', 'breathes'), and مَرَّةً glossed 'times' where it means 'once'. The pattern is that the day's chosen sense for a function word (لَا, مَا) overrides the sense the line needs. One engine issue: tanwīn before a hamzat-waṣl imperative gives 'waghadan dhhab'. No round-1 fix in this chunk introduced a new problem.
