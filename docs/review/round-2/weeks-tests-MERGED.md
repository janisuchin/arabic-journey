# weeks-tests: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: I read all 2,055 lines of audit/weeks-tests.txt: 13 week tests (128 items), titles, titleSegs and levels. I checked every answer key against the Arabic and the English options. All 128 keys are correct, and no distractor is also correct (the closest is w11t7 options[3], a direct quotation, listed as a note). No harakat, case, agreement or verb-form errors were found in the Arabic. The remaining problems are tap meanings. I checked 822 tap glosses across 442 tap lines and 17 were wrong or misleading for their line. Two are major: مَا glossed 'what' in the negation item w6t4, and أَكْبَرُ glossed 'bigger' in the superlative item w9t9; in both the gloss points to the distractor. The minor ones are mostly a sense borrowed from another lesson (هُنَاكَ 'there is', إِنَّ 'that (after qāla)', السَّاعَة 'the clock', حَارًّا 'after was', '(after an)' on verbs that follow lan). There is also one engine pattern: proclitics before words without al- are not hyphenated in the transliteration. Totals: 13 findings (2 major, 11 minor), 6 notes.
