# days-51-60: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: I read the whole days-51-60 export: every Arabic line, every seg, every tap list, quiz and mistake card. I also checked the matching entries in glossary-mistakes.txt (أَجْلِسُ, جَوَازِي, تُوقِظُنَا, لِلَيْلَتَانِ, زَارْتُمُ, نَزُورْهَا), and all of them describe the mistake correctly. The Arabic of these ten days is clean. The lan + subjunctive, li- of purpose, kāna/yakūnu + -an, ordinal dates, dual after prepositions, hollow-verb shortening (zurtu, lam nazurhā) and object suffixes are all taught and used correctly, and every answer key is right. I found 9 errors: 1 major and 8 minor. The one major is a tap gloss on day 51: تَأْتِي is glossed '(they) come (f)' in 'Layla comes'. Five minor tap glosses show another sense of the word: لِـ = 'for; belonging to' on the purpose-li day, بِخَيْر quoting wa-anti inside the masculine phrase, لَا = 'don't' on an indicative verb, سَتَكُونُ = 'there will be' for a copula, and أَذْهَبَ '(after an)' in a lan line. There are also two minor Arabic points: a bare حَارَّة after تَكُونُ, against the day's own rule, and a mistake card whose wrong and right sides transliterate the same. One engine issue: '-·hu'/'-·hā' for bare suffixes. Tap glosses checked: about 668 tap tokens (266 tap lines). 12 were wrong or misleading: تَأْتِي 1, لِـ 4, بِخَيْر 3, لَا 1, سَتَكُونُ 2, أَذْهَبَ 1. About 650 were correct. The round-1 fixes I could see in this range (al-yawmu dates, -ayni after li-, hamzat-waṣl spellings) introduced no new problems.
