The Arabic of the lessons is clean, and every answer key is correct. Errors: 6, 0 major and 6 minor. (1) الْوَاحِدَة is glossed 'the first (f)'; it is the cardinal, and 'first' is الْأُولَى. (2) The hamzat-waṣl nouns after al- have the kasra on the alif (الاِثْنَيْن, الاِسْتِرَاحَة) instead of on the lām, which also differs from the course's own later spelling. (3–4) Two English lines add intensifiers ('very', 'really'). (5) The engine turns Eastern Arabic digits into Western ones in ⟶, which garbles the day-7 digits lesson and gives away quiz d7q2. (6) The new rule that names bare letters in prose produces 'hamza hamza, tāʾ marbūṭa tāʾ marbūṭa'. Tap glosses: I checked 493 tap glosses (270 tap lines, 0 ∅), and 1 was wrong (الْوَاحِدَة in d10q7). The same wrong gloss is in 2 segs. All the other taps fit the sense of their line. Five notes.
