# scenarios-1: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: I read all of audit/scenarios-1.txt: 16 scenarios (cafe to weather-plans), each with its dialogue, segs, mistakes, quizzes and photo-scene spots. I checked harakat, case endings (tanwīn after verbs, kāna/laysa predicates, lam/lan/an moods, imperatives and prohibitives), gender and number agreement (number-noun polarity with جُنَيْه، رَغِيف، كَعْكَة، دَقِيقَة، مِلْعَقَة، سَنَة، دَرَجَة; non-human plurals; addressee gender for -ka/-ki), translations and quiz keys. The Arabic of the dialogues is in very good shape: I found no wrong case ending, conjugation, agreement or answer key, and the round-1 fixes (mistake cards, -ayni and dual rules) read correctly. All 12 errors are glosses or transliteration (3 major, 9 minor). (1) The tap on طَوِيلَةً in holiday-story mistakes[0].right says 'tall (f, after 'is not')' on a kāna card. (2) الْإِشَارَة is tapped as 'pointing' on the traffic-light photo spot. (3) الْوَاحِدَة is glossed 'the first (f)' in the appointment scenario. (4) The minor ones are context-blind taps (مِنْ='from' for 'than', لَا='not' for prohibitive 'don't', مَرَّةً='once' after كَمْ, هُوَ='it'/'he' mix-ups, مَقْهَى '(of)', الْجَبَل '(s)', تَفَضَّلْ='here you are' where the text says 'please sit/come in'), plus one engine pattern: a construct tāʾ marbūṭa with no vowel is romanised without its -t (qiṭʿa kaʿk, thalātha danānīr). Tap-gloss check: 390 tap lines with 660 tap glosses checked; 18 tap glosses were wrong or misleading in context (covered by 11 findings), plus 4 seg glosses of الْوَاحِدَة.
