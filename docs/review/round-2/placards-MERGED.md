# placards: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: I checked all 20 placards in audit/placards.txt: every Arabic line, its harakat, case endings, agreement, the transliteration, the segs, and all 65 tap lines (116 tap glosses). The Arabic is in very good shape. There are no wrong case endings, agreement or verb forms: numbers polarity (thalāthatu awlādin), the diptote ḥamrāʾu, lam/lan moods, laysa, the demonstratives and the sun/moon lists are all correct. I found 6 errors (1 major, 5 minor). The patterns are tap glosses taken from the wrong sense of a particle or ending: مَا = 'what' on the mā-negation note (major); object-pronoun glosses on the possessive placard; a 'nationality ending' gloss on colours; a he-form gloss on the past stem. There is also one leftover hamzat-waṣl spelling (الاِثْنَيْن) and one engine glitch ('-·hu'). Tap glosses checked: 116, wrong or misleading: 11 (مَا 1, possessive endings 7, nisba endings 2, past stem 1).
