# vocabulary: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: Read the whole vocabulary export (809 entries): every headword, plural, form, gender, example, note and every tap gloss. The Arabic itself is in very good shape: genders, plurals (broken and sound), verb paradigms, imperatives, jussives/subjunctives, number agreement, elatives and the passive forms are correct; I found only two spelling problems (انْظُرُوا without its u, read Inẓurū; الاِثْنَيْن with the kasra on the waṣl alif). The errors are almost all tap glosses. I checked 6,010 tap glosses; about 170 are wrong or misleading for their line: ~65 context errors (a gloss from another sentence's sense: مَا=what in a negative, الْإِشَارَة=pointing, اللَّيْلَة=tonight for 'per night', أَحْسَنُ=the best for 'better', تُحِبُّ=you like for 'she loves', رِحْلَتُنَا=our flight, أَوَّلَ='the day before yesterday' in 'the first lesson', السَّاعَةَ=at the hour for 'the clock', كَرِيم=Karim in رَمَضَان كَرِيم, سَادَة=plain coffee, رِجَال=men (toilets); plus grammar tags such as 'after laysa', 'after was', 'form after lam', 'part of fifteen' attached to lines where they are false), and ~105 auto-composed form glosses that keep the headword's tense/person and append the label (يَطْبُخُ=cooked (present), نَامِي=sleeps (order, to a woman), حِمَايَة=protects (verbal noun)), reported once as an engine finding.
