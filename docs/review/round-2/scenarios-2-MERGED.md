# scenarios-2: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: I read the whole scenarios-2 chunk: 16 scenarios (airport-checkin to school), with their dialogues, segs, mistake cards and quizzes, plus their scene photo spots. I checked harakat and case, agreement, verb moods after an/lan/lam/lā, number–noun rules (3–10 polarity, 11–99 tamyīz, hundreds), the totals in the dialogues against the quiz keys, and every tap gloss. The dialogue Arabic is in very good shape: number agreement, dual forms, moods and the gender of address are correct throughout, and all quiz answer keys and mistake-card rules are right. I found 17 errors (3 major, 14 minor). The majors are all tap glosses: شَرِيحَة/شَرَائِح is glossed as 'slide' in the SIM-card scenario (two findings), and the correct mistake card's أَفْضَلُ is glossed as 'the best'. The minors are mostly tap glosses that take a sense from another context: مِنْ 'from' for 'than', الْمَكْتَب 'office' for counter or desk, الْعَرَبَة 'carriage', هُوَ 'he' for 'it', سَيِّدَات 'toilets', the tourist-police hints. The others are an inconsistent accusative صَبَاحَ الْخَيْر in two scenarios, أَيَّهُمَا for أَيُّهُمَا, an inconsistent مَحْفَظَة/مِحْفَظَة, a ẕ typo, and one engine issue: tanwīn before waṣla is rendered 'taqrīran l-āna' instead of 'taqrīrani l-āna'. Tap glosses checked: 818 word glosses on 366 tap lines; 23 of them are wrong or misleading in their line (all covered by the gloss findings above).
