# glossary-auto-1: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: I checked all 3,657 vocalised keys in audit/glossary-auto-1.txt (ء to زَبَائِن) against the exact form of each key. 14 findings: 1 major (زَارَنَا glossed 'they visited us' though the form is 'he visited us') and 13 minor. Most of the errors follow patterns. About 60 template glosses put the English in the wrong tense, mood or part of speech: past verbs glossed in the present ('sends (past)'), imperatives glossed as 'runs/sits/to drink (order …)', verbal nouns glossed as verbs ('protects (verbal noun)'), and 1st/2nd-person presents glossed in the 3rd person. Nine 3rd-person feminine singular forms are glossed 'they', copied from sentences with non-human plurals. Nine mistake-only forms still carry the '(wrong — correct: …)' note as their meaning, several opening with a plain meaning ('I can', 'we visited'). There are also single slips: a transliteration given as the meaning (الْمَلْعَبَ), الْوَاحِدَة glossed 'the first', a wrong '-a after an' tag on an indicative, singular الْجَبَل glossed '(s)', 'room' for دَوْرَة, and 'Harb' for حَرْب. In total about 95 tap glosses were wrong (mostly the minor template group); the rest were correct for their form.
