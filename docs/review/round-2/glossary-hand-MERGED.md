# glossary-hand: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: Checked every entry of the hand glossary (about 395 vocalised forms) and all 46 mistake notes (441 tap glosses in total), grepping content/ for forms used in several senses and reading every mistake card whose note looked doubtful. 6 errors (2 major, 4 minor): the case-ending gloss for ـُ teaches that the first word of an iḍāfa always ends in -u; أَبُوهُ teaches 'after a verb use abāhu' (it is the object form); the dual mistake notes say 'wrong after a verb' instead of 'as an object'; the لِأَدْعُو note's correct form does not match the card; the إِنْتَرْنِت mistake note describes a mistake no card contains (bare internet is correct in the course); and the engine shows -·hu/-·hā/-·hum for the bare object endings. The other glosses, including months, ordinals, time expressions, clitic and number forms, are correct for every place the form appears. Pattern: the wrong glosses state a rule that ties a case to a position ('first word of a chain', 'after a verb') when it really depends on the word's role.
