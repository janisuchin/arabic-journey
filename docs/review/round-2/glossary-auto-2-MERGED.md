# glossary-auto-2: round findings (merged A + B)

Reviewer A: Checked all 3,657 vocalised keys in audit/glossary-auto-2.txt (zāy to yāʾ) against the exact form. 63 tap glosses are wrong for their form, grouped into 10 findings (3 major, 7 minor). Major: 18 present-tense verbs glossed in the past tense ('laughed (present)', 'was written (present passive)'), صَنَعَ glossed 'was made', شَابَّة glossed 'young man'. Minor patterns: past verbs with present English plus '(past)', finite verbs as English infinitives, imperatives as 3rd person, singular verbs glossed 'they' from verb-first sentences, وَإِنَّ as 'and that', context-only senses (مِنْهُ 'at him'), nouns of unity glossed as plurals. Nouns, pronoun suffixes, case labels, numbers and letter names were otherwise consistently correct.
